# Topic 1: LLM Basics

Goal: understand the basic ideas behind Large Language Models so AI interview questions stop feeling random.

Study slowly in this order:

1. What is an LLM?
2. What are tokens?
3. What is a context window?
4. What are embeddings?
5. What is the Transformer architecture?
6. What is attention?
7. What are prompts and system prompts?
8. Why do hallucinations happen?
9. What is grounding?
10. How these basics connect to RAG, agents, caching, and inference.

Use this folder like a notebook:

- `lesson-01-from-scratch.md`: first beginner lesson.
- `interview-question-map.md`: which uploaded questions depend on Topic 1.
- `practice.md`: small exercises, no full solutions.
- `glossary.md`: simple definitions.
